# Momako Onboarding Guide (English)

> This guide is for **game designers, product managers, artists, and anyone with a game idea** who wants to prototype without writing boilerplate from scratch.

---

## What is this?

A rapid-prototype scaffold for **Game Jams**:
- Built-in start menu, settings, how-to-play, and credits.
- A functional 2D platformer reference implementation.
- In-game pause menu, settings modal, and audio controls.
- Automatic canvas scaling and resolution presets.

---

## Vibe Coding with AI

You don't need to learn the whole engine architecture:
1. Describe your game idea in `docs/spec/spec.md`.
2. Ask your AI coding assistant (Cursor, Claude, Copilot) to implement mechanics in `src/scenes/game-scene.ts`.
3. Check HMR results immediately in the browser and tune the game feel iteratively!
